Chicken Fried Rice

Description

This chicken fried rice is simple, comforting, and surprisingly restaurant-quality. It’s the kind of dish that tastes like it took a lot more effort than it actually did—rich, buttery, and full of bold, savory flavor. I picked it up while working at a Korean spot, where I learned that with the right touch, even the most basic ingredients can come together into something special. It’s quick, honest food that hits every time.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably day-old)
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, diced or shredded
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup diced carrot and onion
  • 1.5 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced (for garnish)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

    • Steps

    1. Assemble ingredients
    2. Heat pan on low flame and add vegetable oil and butter
    3. Once melted add egg and slowly stir until lightly cooked
    4. Reduce flame. Add chicken, Carrot, and onion to wok and toss til combined and vegestables are sweated
    5. Add your rice to the wok
    6. Increase flame and begin mashing/seperating rice occasionally tossing to prevent burning
    7. Once throuroughly seperated and nearly done rice heat to a low simmer and add your fish sauce to taste
    8. Toss on high heat while simultainiously mixing to ensure the seasoning is well distributed
    9. Remove from heat, season with salt and pepper, garnish with sliced green onions and serve
    10. Enjoy your delicious chicken fried rice!
